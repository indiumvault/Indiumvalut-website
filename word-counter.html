Quick start

How to use it

  1. Open the Word & character counter in IndiumVault Labs.
  2. Paste or type your text into the text area.
  3. Read the live word, character, sentence, and reading-time estimates below it.
Try it now ↗

What can you use it for?

  • Check essay length before submitting.
  • Keep a social post within a platform's character limit.
  • Estimate how long a paragraph may take to read.

Privacy and practical tips

This guide links to a browser-based tool hosted by IndiumVault Labs. The tool is designed to work without an account. For sensitive information, use your own trusted device and review the tool's behaviour before entering private data.

Frequently asked questions

Does it upload my text?

No. The counter runs in your browser and does not need an account or upload.

How is reading time estimated?

It estimates about 200 words per minute, so actual reading time may vary.

Are spaces counted as characters?

Yes. The character count includes spaces and punctuation.
