About the project / Independent by design

Useful things.
Less friction.

IndiumVault is an independent project built around a simple idea: the internet should offer practical tools and clear learning resources without making every small task complicated.

01 / FREE UTILITIES

Tools that get to the point

Use writing helpers, text converters, JSON formatting, generators, calculations, and everyday conversions directly in your browser. No account is required for the current tools.

02 / STUDY & PROGRESS

A workspace for consistency

Launchpad brings tasks, a focus timer, revision targets, and a weekly timetable into one place. Your planner is stored locally in your browser, not in an IndiumVault account.

03 / LEARN BY DOING

Guides with practical examples

Our guides aim to explain a task clearly, show common mistakes, and link straight to the relevant tool so you can put the idea into practice.

04 / TRANSPARENT BY DEFAULT

No fake promises

IndiumVault is a work in progress. We do not promise income, guaranteed search rankings, or perfect results. Check important outputs before relying on them.

OUR PRINCIPLES

Small, useful, honest improvements

  • Mobile-friendly pages that work without installing an app.
  • Free hosting and lightweight pages wherever practical.
  • Privacy-conscious browser tools and clear explanations of local storage.
  • Original guides that solve a specific problem rather than filler written only for search engines.
  • Accessibility basics such as keyboard focus states and reduced-motion support.
