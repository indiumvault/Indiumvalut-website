Frequently asked questions

What is a UUID?

A UUID is a 128-bit identifier commonly represented as groups of hexadecimal characters separated by hyphens.

Is every UUID guaranteed unique?

No identifier system can promise absolute uniqueness, but random UUID v4 collisions are extremely unlikely when generated correctly.

Can I use it for security tokens?

Use a purpose-built, reviewed token-generation approach for authentication or other security-critical systems.
