Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

This page explains how the current IndiumVault website and its browser-based tools handle information. It is a plain-language summary, not legal advice.

1. Information you enter into tools

The current Labs tools are designed to run in your browser. Text you type into the word counter, text converter, JSON formatter, unit converter, password generator, and UUID generator is processed locally by the page and is not intentionally sent to an IndiumVault server.

2. Launchpad planner

The Launchpad stores your task list, weekly checkpoints, revision targets (subject, topic, target date, and completion status), and weekly timetable in your browser’s local storage on your device. This information is not sent to an IndiumVault server. Clearing browser site data may remove it. The focus timer works in the page and does not need an account. Use the same browser on the same device to see your saved planner; private browsing or browser storage restrictions may prevent persistence.

3. Feedback

If you choose “Prepare feedback,” the site opens a GitHub issue draft. Any information you submit there is handled by GitHub and may be public according to GitHub’s settings and policies. Do not include passwords, private information, or sensitive personal details in feedback. The copy option keeps the text on your device until you choose where to paste it.

4. Cookies and analytics

The site currently does not intentionally set advertising cookies or run a first-party analytics service. GitHub Pages and your browser may process technical information needed to deliver and secure the site; consult GitHub’s policies for details.

5. External services and links

Links to GitHub and other external sites are governed by those services’ own privacy practices. IndiumVault does not control their data handling.

6. Children and personal information

Please do not submit private or sensitive information through the site. The current tools do not require an account. If the site’s features or data practices change, this policy should be updated to reflect those changes.

7. Changes and contact

This policy may change as IndiumVault evolves. For questions or suggestions, use the feedback section or visit the GitHub repository.
