Quick start

How to use it

  1. Open the Text Case Converter in IndiumVault Labs.
  2. Paste or type the text you want to change.
  3. Choose UPPERCASE, lowercase, Title Case, or Sentence case, then copy the result.

What can you use it for?

  • Standardize headings and document titles.
  • Clean up text copied from different sources.
  • Prepare consistent labels and sample text.

Privacy and practical tips

This guide links to a browser-based tool hosted by IndiumVault Labs. The tool is designed to work without an account. For sensitive information, use your own trusted device and review the tool's behaviour before entering private data.

Frequently asked questions

Does conversion change the meaning?

It changes capitalization only, though names and acronyms may need manual review.

Is my text stored?

The converter processes text in the current browser page; avoid entering sensitive information on shared devices.

Can I undo a conversion?

You can edit the text area again or paste your original text back in.
